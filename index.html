Features

Built for teams that ship, not teams that meet

Every feature exists to remove a specific kind of friction — not to add another dashboard nobody opens.

Deadlines that don't hide in chat

Every task carries its own due date and owner, visible on one timeline — so "I thought someone else had it" stops happening.

One board, zero status meetings

Automated daily digests summarize what moved and what's stuck, replacing the 30-minute standup with a 2-minute read.

Ownership that's actually visible

Assign a single directly-responsible person to every task, so accountability is a fact on the board, not a debate in a meeting.
